Two kinds of arrows

Change the design. Then compare source imports with runtime calls.

Arrows mean “source code imports definitions from.”

SQLite adaptersqlite_stock.py

Both sides refer to policy-owned definitions.

The adapter also imports sqlite3. The policy does not import either the adapter or its driver.

Setup imports policy and adapter, supplies the object, and owns the connection.

How to read this visual

Read left to right, or top to bottom on a narrow display. In the inverted source view, the adapter's arrow points back to the contract.

Key insight

Policy can call a concrete object without importing its implementation. Injection and source dependency direction are different questions.

Simplification or limitation

Conceptual maps of one successful read. The contract is not a runtime relay. Errors, concurrency, and most setup imports are omitted.
