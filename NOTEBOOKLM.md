# NotebookLM Handoff

Use NotebookLM for retrieval practice, comparison flashcards, scenario quizzes, and mock interviews after unit notes are Approved.

## Upload

Upload:

- approved unit `README.md` files;
- the relevant part of [CURRICULUM.md](CURRICULUM.md);
- the chosen section of [LEARNING_PATHS.md](LEARNING_PATHS.md);
- approved completed-project design explanations when useful;
- [docs/SOURCE_AND_VERSION_POLICY.md](docs/SOURCE_AND_VERSION_POLICY.md) for history, version, or Python-mechanism-heavy notebooks.

Do not upload draft notes, `PROGRESS.md`, `REVIEW.md`, raw attempts, solutions, test output, generated code noise, caches, environments, private data, or copyrighted source material.

## Best uses

- Generate flashcards from `Physical Notebook Core`.
- Ask scenario questions that require choosing or rejecting a pattern.
- Compare commonly confused patterns.
- Run path-specific mixed quizzes.
- Run one-question-at-a-time senior interviews.

Bring discovered weaknesses back to the existing dedicated unit chat. NotebookLM never updates progress, decides mastery, or replaces authoritative sources.

Detailed prompts are in [docs/NOTEBOOKLM.md](docs/NOTEBOOKLM.md).
